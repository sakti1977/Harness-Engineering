# Scope Contract

Every non-trivial task defines:

1. **Outcome** — one externally observable behavior.
2. **Expected surface** — files/modules likely to change.
3. **Exclusions** — related work not funded by the task.
4. **Evidence** — commands/assertions that prove completion.
5. **Discovery rule** — treatment of necessary or interesting work found outside scope.

## Rules
- Necessary dependency outside the surface → amend scope with evidence.
- Useful but non-required work → queue it.
- Do not turn cleanup, renaming, formatting, or unrelated refactoring into task completion.
- Completion is based on behavior and evidence, not file count.
